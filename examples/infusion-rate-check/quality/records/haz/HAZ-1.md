## Hazard
Excess drug delivery energy, in the form of a wrong infusion rate.

## Sequence of events
A nurse enters a rate ten times the intended one; nothing stops the start; the infusion runs.

## Hazardous situation
The patient is exposed to a rate far above the prescription.

## Harm
Overdose: serious injury in the worst case, limited to non-serious injury by the pump's hardware maximum in this example.
