## Statement
A nurse must be warned before starting an infusion whose rate exceeds the prescribed limit, so that an entry error is caught before it reaches the patient.

## Rationale
Rate entry errors are a known source of overdose. A warning at the point of entry is the last moment the nurse can catch one.

## Validation method
Summative usability test with nurses in a simulated ward.
