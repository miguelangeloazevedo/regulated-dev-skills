# Fictional handoff: fixed software, pending quality-event effectiveness

**Illustration only. No product was run, no containment was performed, no reportability decision was made and no person approved these records.** The external event/action labels below are explanatory references, not standalone record types.

A fictional complaint describes a stale schedule displayed after reconnecting. The originating complaint and nonconformity remain in the manufacturer's controlled QMS. `PRB-1` references them and records the observed version, conditions, awareness date and uncertainty. A proposed interim-protection decision goes to the responsible owner without waiting for reproduction. Nothing here claims the display issue is or is not safety relevant.

Investigation asks three different questions: which state transition allowed stale data (occurrence), why disconnect/reconnect checks missed it (escape), and which other screens or products share the state-management component (systemic scope). These remain hypotheses until evidence supports them. A link to an old investigation is a starting point, not a proved cause.

If a change is authorised, `CR-1` addresses `PRB-1`; the controlled requirement/design changes and relevant `TC`/`TR` records retain actual before/after evidence. In this illustration **all execution evidence is not run**. Do not copy a pass or a human approval into a real project from this narrative.

Even after a future verified software fix, the external corrective-action plan could still have a pending monitoring window for recurrence and review of the shared release-check process. Implementation verification is not that effectiveness result. The complaint owner, CAPA owner and vigilance owner retain their separate decisions. No unelapsed monitoring period or absence of new reports is automatically a pass.

If no reproduction is obtained, retain the observations and attempts, stop speculative code fixing and continue protection/safety assessment. An authorised no-software-change disposition would need a supported rationale, unresolved uncertainty and external-action references. It cannot be invented to close a safety-relevant report.
